

# **Data Scientist - Take Home Assignment** 

## **A. The Business Scenario** 

**Customer churn** is traditional usecase on Telco Industry (or any industry). Obviously we have built one and have the model as our basic pillars or user retentions. You are to relook the entire problem and generate a novel approach to this problems. 

This assignment is designed to understand how you think, not how many techniques you can apply. 

## **B. Your Task & Key Deliverables** 

Design an end-to-end data-driven approach to identify churn risk, determine the most appropriate retention action, and explain how the resulting intelligence could be operationalized through business workflows and/or an agentic capability. 

The core of the assignment remains the analytical and Data Science problem. We are also interested in whether you can design outputs that are usable by downstream decisioning, AI/BI, or agentic systems. 

You are provided with customer profile, usage, and support interaction data. You may assume the data is imperfect, as in real-world scenarios. 

Assume your analytical solution will eventually be consumed by a retention workflow, business user, or AI agent. 

Explain how you would expose the intelligence generated by your solution so that the downstream system can make safe and useful decisions. 

You may consider: 

- What outputs the model should produce beyond a churn probability. 

- How the system decides who to act on, what action to take, and when not to act. 

- Whether the decision should be deterministic, model-driven, LLM-assisted, or a combination. 

- What context an agent would need before recommending or executing a retention action. 

- Which actions could be automated versus which should require human approval. 

- Guardrails, confidence thresholds, customer eligibility, cost constraints, and conflicting signals. 

- How outcomes are captured so the model / decisioning logic can learn and improve over time. 

- How you would monitor both model performance and agent/workflow performance. 

You are not expected to build a production agent. A clear architecture, workflow, pseudocode, or prototype is sufficient. 

### **The Datasets You'll Use** 

You will be provided with three datasets representing different facets of our customer relationships: 

1. **customer_base.csv** : Core customer profile and contract information. 

   - customerId, gender, age, partner, dependents, region, contractType, paperlessBilling, paymentMethod, churnDate (null if not churned). 

2. **service_usage.csv** : Monthly customer usage and billing data. Each customer has multiple rows. 

   - customerId, yearMonth, tenureMonths, monthlyCharges, totalCharges, hasPhoneService, hasInternetService, dataUsageGB. 

3. **support_interactions.csv** : A log of all customer interactions with our support center. 

   - customerId, interactionDate, interactionType (e.g., email, call, chat), supportTopic (e.g., ‘billing_inquiry’, ‘technical_issue’, ‘contract_question’), resolutionStatus (e.g., ‘resolved’, ‘pending’).
